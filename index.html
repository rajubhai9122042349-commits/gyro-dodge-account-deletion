Gyro Dodge - Account Deletion

This page explains how Gyro Dodge users can request deletion of their game account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to 2muchfun9122@gmail.com .
  2. Use the subject line: Gyro Dodge Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your in-game player ID and the Google account email associated with your game account, if applicable.
  4. The developer may request reasonable information to verify that you own the account.

You do not need to provide your Google password or any verification code by email.

Data That Will Be Deleted

After your request is verified and processed, the developer will delete your game account and associated data that can be identified and deleted, including:

  • Account and player profile information.
  • Player name and associated account identifiers.
  • Leaderboard scores and player records associated with the deleted account.

Deletion of an account and associated data is subject to the actual data stored by the game and its service providers.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain records may be retained when required by applicable law or when necessary for security, fraud prevention, or resolving legal disputes. Such data will be retained only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose.

Deletion Processing Time

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days. If additional time is necessary, the user will be informed where appropriate.

Delete Data Without Deleting Your Account

To request deletion of specific data without deleting your entire account, contact the developer using the email below. Your request will be assessed according to applicable law and the game's data retention requirements.

Contact the Developer

For account deletion or data deletion requests, contact: 2muchfun9122@gmail.com
